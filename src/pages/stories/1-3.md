Major changes are taking place in Grande Prairie's legal system this month. On October 21st at 6:00am, the brand new Grande Prairie Police Service officially took over policing inside city limits. Don't worry, if you need help, you can still dial the same emergency service numbers and the usual non-emergency line (780-830-5700). Alongside this change, the province added an extra $2M to the final stage, which includes the Public Safety Communications Centre; It will now handle dispatch calls locally. This transition began in 2024, with the RCMP and GPPS officers working together. This decision was ultimately made with desire for the city to have more control over policing, staffing, and spending, as well as working closer with vulnerable populations and our community. While the RCMP will still watch over the surrounding county, the Clairmont RCMP detachment will remain in the city. Keep an eye out for GPPS uniforms and vehicles around town as they keep Grande Prairie safe.
