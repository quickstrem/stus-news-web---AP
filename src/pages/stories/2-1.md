November 11 saw multiple Remembrance day events across Grande Prairie. An indoor ceremony was held in the Bonnets Energy Centre and an outdoor ceremony was held at the cenotaph near Legion Branch 54. Many smaller events were also held at schools around the city and in the surrounding area. November 11 also concluded the Royal Canadian Legion’s Poppy Campaign. While figures for this year are not out yet, the campaign often makes close to 20 million dollars annually that are given to the Poppy Trust Fund to “help support our veterans and their families.”
