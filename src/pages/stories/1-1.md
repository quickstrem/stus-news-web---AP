A number of students from St. John Paul II Catholic High School participated in a walkout on Octover 13th. The walkout was in protest of a social media post shared by Amy Davidson that was originally made by the PPC party leader Maxime Bernier claiming that "The residential-school 'genocide' claim is a hoax." Davidson has responded claiming "[she is] not a residential-school denialist." Despite this, she has been unassigned from teaching duties. One of the grandmothers of the students, Ann Maje, applauded the students and described attending residential schools as "horrific."
