The Microsoft outage that began on Thursday, November 12th has interrupted communication and email for many worldwide. Microsoft has come out to say that they are "deeply sorry" and that the outage was caused by "technical issues," not a cybersecurity attack. Services have already broadly recovered though costs worldwide are estimated to range in the billions.
